# TM WerX red team, 7 November 2026

Scope: the live set-up at tmwerx.co.uk (checked from outside), the full code (an independent security review), and
every screen of the public site, the office app and the crew app on iPhone (SE, 15, 15 landscape, 15 Pro Max),
Android (Pixel 7, Galaxy S9+), iPad (Mini, Pro 11 portrait and landscape) and Android tablet (Galaxy Tab S4 portrait
and landscape). Everything below is fixed, with a test that tries it again (test/tm.py, test/devices.py).

## Live set-up (outside view)
- Fine: test-only pages are off, both APIs refuse unsigned requests, share links can't be guessed, http goes to
  https, strict security headers on the pages.
- Fixed: the crew app's files didn't send the "https only" header.

## Security (code review)
| # | Severity | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | A crew login could write records (complete, fail, start) to any visit in its company | Records and photos must be for one of the sender's own visits; the job comes from the visit |
| 2 | High | Photos could close any photo task, or be backdated to look on time | A photo only settles a task on its own visit, taken from 15 minutes before it was due; phone times corrected by the phone's clock error |
| 3 | High | One phone could flood the photo reminders and urgent emails for every company | Repeat photo tasks made once per visit; records rate-limited per person; urgent emails capped per person and company; reminders shared fairly between companies |
| 4 | High (safety) | A phone clock set ahead could push lone-working check-ins days into the future | Phone times are never later than the server's |
| 5 | Medium | Notification addresses could point anywhere (server-side requests to other sites) | Only Apple, Google, Mozilla and Microsoft push services; three phones per person; dropped after 10 failures |
| 6 | Medium | An office user could make a job's evidence be deleted early by changing its dates | Deletion judged by the last visit and the last record, not the job's dates |
| 7 | Medium | A malformed record could break the job pack for good | Records' data cleaned on arrival; the pack tolerates bad lists; one bad record never stops the rest |
| 8 | Medium | Job-request email: no limits, worked after a subscription lapsed, address could never change | 200 an hour per company, refused when the account isn't active, "Give us a new address" in Settings |
| 9 | Medium | Any office user could download the whole company export | Administrators only |
| 10 | Medium | Timesheets could be padded with backdated sign-ins | Phone times believed only for the last 7 days and corrected for clock error |
| 11 | Low-Medium | Shared phones kept the last person's notifications, drawings and jobs | Sign-out (and any server sign-out) clears them; unsent records stay and send when that person signs in again |
| 12 | Low | Crew could be locked out by someone else; crew passwords too short | Only wrong passwords count, per address and username; passwords now three words and three digits |
| 13 | Low | Some ids from the browser weren't checked against the company | Checked, and company added to the remaining queries |
| 14 | Low | A drawing revision name could put files outside the folder in a job zip | Names cleaned |
| 15 | Low | Job pack allowed inline scripts; CSV downloads could run spreadsheet formulas; long lists broke the 100-value database limit | Pack uses a one-time script key; CSV cells made safe; lists passed as one value |

## Phones and tablets
- The office app was wider than the screen on every phone and portrait tablet (titles, tables, board, buttons cut
  off): fixed, with a phone layout (menu strip, the board as a list of days, full-screen dialogs, bigger tap targets).
- iPad portrait: the menu strip stretched to half the screen: fixed.
- Crew app: a red "Signed out" bar appeared straight after signing in, and the sign-in form could be wiped while
  someone was typing: both fixed.
- Crew app in landscape: the Panic button is smaller so it covers less.

## Other end-to-end fixes
- "Today" was worked out in UTC in about a dozen places (Live, board, timesheets, daily reminders, the crew's list),
  so between midnight and 1am in summer the wrong day showed: now UK dates throughout.
- "Photo due" and "Check in now" notifications opened the home screen instead of the visit: fixed.
- Crew logins could open the office Account, Billing and Agreement pages: they now go to the crew app.
- Phone notifications checked against an independent implementation (encryption and signature): correct.
